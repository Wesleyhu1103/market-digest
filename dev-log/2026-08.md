## 2026-08-21 — Guard publish workflow against same-day overwrite
- What: publish-digest now skips current manual dispatches without incoming, re-checks freshness in build, preserves prepared incoming digests, and avoids publish_digest.py on static-only deploys.
- Why: queued/manual/backup runs could regenerate over an already-current same-day digest with no archive, and scheduled generation could delete committed incoming content.
- Gotcha: build-time freshness is the source of truth for Claude generation; plan-time stale can be stale itself while jobs wait in the publish concurrency queue.
