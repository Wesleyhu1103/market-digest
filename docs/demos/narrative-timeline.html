Stagflation Stops Being a Forecast

Markets · Thursday, June 11, 2026

Producer prices climbed 6.5% year over year, the fastest pace since November 2022, and the European Central Bank capitulated with its first hike since 2023. The combination put the stagflation framing — hot prices into a softening labor market — squarely in the tape.

“The textbook stagflation bind is no longer hypothetical; it’s in the print.”
